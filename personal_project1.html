Dead Air

Project Type: Game Jam Project Duration: 72 hours Team size: 2

Project Information

Dead Air is a game I've created for the Dutch Global Game Jam 2026 in Groningen. The theme during this game jam was "Mask". Down below you'll see more about the gameplay!


Game Story: The power plant has malfunctioned, it has released tons of toxic gas. You are a "Breather", a disposable technician sent into the dark tunnels to seal the leaks. But in these depths, it isn't just the dark you have to worry about. The air itself is your greatest enemy. In Dead Air, you must navigate a underground maze where every second counts.

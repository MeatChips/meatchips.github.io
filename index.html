Hi! I’m Gijs Elderman, a Game Programmer and Designer from The Netherlands and I’ve been making games for the past 7 years.


I graduated at Alfa-College Groningen in 2024 as a Software Developer (Creative) and im currently studying at Hanze University of Applied Sciences with the study Creative Media & Game Technologies .


I have done internship at 2 companies so far. At New Game Studios in Groningen, I have worked as a VR/PC Gameplay Programmer and Level Designer for half a year. At Beepy Games in Bedum, I have worked as a UI/UX Programmer, Gameplay Programmer and 3D Artist.

Tech Stack:

  • Languages: C#, GML, HTML, CSS, C++, C, Python, Javascript
